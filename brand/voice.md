# Kettlebird Tea: brand voice guide

Kettlebird Tea is a small online tea shop. We are launching **the Steep Club**, a monthly tea subscription box.

**Who we write to:** people who joined our waitlist. They are busy, aged 25 to 45, and want a calm moment in their day. Some are new to loose-leaf tea, some want to give it as a gift, and some drink tea every day.

## 1. Our voice in three words

**Calm, warm, curious.**

We sound like a friend who knows a lot about tea but never shows off. We write short, friendly sentences. We are never pushy or salesy.

## 2. Words we use

- **Tea words:** steep, brew, cup, leaf, loose-leaf
- **Feeling words:** ritual, pause, slow down, discover
- **Words for the reader:** "you", "your box", "your cup", "your tea moment"
- **The club:** say "the Steep Club" warmly, as a club you belong to.

## 3. Words we avoid

- **Pushy or salesy:** buy now, act now, hurry, last chance, limited time only, don't miss out, exclusive
- **Hype and fancy words:** game-changer, revolutionary, superfood, miracle, guaranteed, artisanal, luxury, cheap
- **Not confirmed yet:** hand-picked, small-batch. Only use these once a supplier confirms they are true.
- **Style rules:** no ALL CAPS, and no more than one exclamation mark in each email.

## 4. Claims we must never make

- **No health claims.** Never say detox, cleanse, boosts your immune system, helps you lose weight, burns fat, cures, prevents illness or reduces stress.
  - It's fine to say "a calm moment" or "a pause in your day". Never say the tea itself reduces stress.
- **No claims we can't prove.** Never say best tea in the world, the finest or number one.
- **No promises the team hasn't confirmed.** Don't promise delivery dates, prices, discounts or caffeine levels unless the team has confirmed them.

## 5. How we sign off

Sign off as the team, not a person, so it still works if someone leaves. Use exactly these two lines:

```
Happy steeping,
The Kettlebird team
```

- No P.S.
- Nothing comes after the sign-off except the email footer.
