# Handoff: email 01 subject line angle

- **Tea-curious beginners:** an easy, low-effort start. They worry they can't brew loose-leaf or won't like it.
- **Gift buyers:** a thoughtful gift that's easy to send. They worry it won't arrive in time and want to choose how many months.
- **Daily tea drinkers:** something new to discover. They ask "Will it be better than what I already buy?"
- **Angle A (calm daily ritual):** a pause in your day, e.g. "Your daily pause starts here".
- **Angle B (discovering new teas):** a new tea to try, e.g. "Discover a new tea each month".
- **Pick: Angle A.** Email 01 goes to everyone, and a calm pause is the one thing all three groups want. It also matches the campaign name, "Your Monthly Pause".
- Angle B speaks mainly to daily drinkers, so save it for their version of email 02 ("What's in your first box").
