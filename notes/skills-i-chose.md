# Skills I chose

**Use these:** For writing campaigns use anthropics/knowledge-work-plugins (campaign-plan and email-sequence), and for sending email use resend/resend-skills (resend), because both come from the official companies, are updated regularly, and sit in well-starred repos (35K and 211 stars, with Resend's react-email at 20K).

**Avoid these:** I would skip caffeinelabs/skills@extension-email-marketing, which claims 30K installs but its repo has only 2 stars, and claude-office-skills/skills@email-marketing, which uses "Claude" in its name without being from Anthropic and hasn't been updated since January 2026.

**How I decided:** Before installing any skill, check who publishes it (the official company or an unknown account), whether its GitHub stars and recent updates match its install count, and read its SKILL.md and any scripts yourself to see what it will actually do.
