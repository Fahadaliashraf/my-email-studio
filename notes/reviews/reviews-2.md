# Kettlebird Tea Sampler Box: customer reviews (2 of 6)

> Made-up practice data. Kettlebird and every reviewer below are fictional.

**21. Grace N. — ★★★★★ (5/5)**
Silver Needle and Jasmine Pearl are worth the price of the box on their own. Lovely clean flavours, no bitterness at all if you follow the temperature guide.

**22. Liam R. — ★★☆☆☆ (2/5)**
Half the box was black teas. I specifically picked this because it said "a journey through every kind of tea". Felt more like a journey through breakfast blends.

**23. Zara Q. — ★★★★☆ (4/5)**
Hibiscus Berry iced is a summer revelation. Hot it was a bit much. Box was slightly dented on arrival but everything inside was fine.

**24. Callum E. — ★★★☆☆ (3/5)**
The tea's good. The website said 3–5 working days and it took nine. An email to say it was delayed would have been nice.

**25. Anya V. — ★★★★★ (5/5)**
I've tried a few sampler boxes and this is the one where every tea felt considered. The Oolong Mist is buttery and floral, I didn't know tea could taste like that.

**26. Jack P. — ★☆☆☆☆ (1/5)**
Opened the box to find the Ginger Lemon tin completely empty. Not a single sachet. Sealed tin, nothing inside. Got a refund for that tea but not the postage.

**27. Imogen T. — ★★★★☆ (4/5)**
Really enjoyed it. Would love a version without the Smoky Lapsang, which nobody in my house would touch. It's taking up space in the cupboard now.

**28. Sami A. — ★★★☆☆ (3/5)**
The brewing card uses a little kettle icon system that I just could not decode. Three kettles means hotter? Longer? I gave up and Googled each tea.

**29. Bethany L. — ★★★★★ (5/5)**
Chamomile Honey is like a hug. Bought a second box for my sister. Delivery was quick both times.

**30. Ryan C. — ★★☆☆☆ (2/5)**
Too much packaging, not enough tea. Each tin had three sachets. That's three cups. For the price I expected more like five or six of each.

**31. Fatima Z. — ★★★★☆ (4/5)**
Sencha Spring has a lovely grassy sweetness. One star off because the Sencha card says "boiling water" and the tin label says "70°C". Which is it?

**32. Harry B. — ★★★★★ (5/5)**
Gave this to my dad, who only drinks builder's tea. He now has opinions about Earl Grey Lavender. Thank you, I think.

**33. Leila M. — ★★★☆☆ (3/5)**
Nice idea, mixed execution. The Rooibos Chai and Hedgerow Mint were lovely. The Morning Ember tasted a bit flat, maybe stale?

**34. Nathan F. — ★★★★☆ (4/5)**
Shipping was slow (a week) but the box arrived in perfect condition and the teas were super fresh. I'd order again, just earlier.

**35. Ruth O. — ★★☆☆☆ (2/5)**
Smelled lovely, tasted mostly of cardboard. I think the box sat in a warm depot for a while. Disappointed given the reviews.

**36. Josh K. — ★★★★★ (5/5)**
Ginger Lemon Rescue got me through a horrible week of colds in the office. Strong, zingy, not too sweet. The whole box was a winner.

**37. Amira S. — ★★★★☆ (4/5)**
Very pretty box. I wish there was more of the Jasmine Pearl. I'd trade all the Hibiscus Berry for one more sachet.

**38. Connor W. — ★☆☆☆☆ (1/5)**
Delivery company left it in the rain. Box disintegrated. Sachets were soaked. Kettlebird blamed the courier, the courier blamed Kettlebird. Still waiting on a resolution.

**39. Daisy J. — ★★★★☆ (4/5)**
Lovely selection, but the instructions are on a single card that I lost on day one. Printing the brew time on each tin would solve that.

**40. Yusuf B. — ★★★★★ (5/5)**
Exceptional. The Oolong Mist and Silver Needle are better than what I've had in specialist tea shops. Arrived in three days, well packed.
