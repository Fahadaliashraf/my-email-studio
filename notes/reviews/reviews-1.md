# Kettlebird Tea Sampler Box: customer reviews (1 of 6)

> Made-up practice data. Kettlebird and every reviewer below are fictional.

**1. Priya S. — ★★★★☆ (4/5)**
Loved the Jasmine Pearl, it unfurls beautifully in the cup. Only two pearls' worth of sachets though, which felt stingy when it was clearly the star of the box.

**2. Tom H. — ★★☆☆☆ (2/5)**
Took eleven days to arrive. By the time it got here I'd forgotten I ordered it. Tea itself was fine, the Morning Ember was a decent strong breakfast cup.

**3. Aisha R. — ★★★★★ (5/5)**
Bought this as a gift for my mum and she's been texting me about the Earl Grey Lavender every day since. Gorgeous box, lovely little cards for each tea.

**4. Gareth L. — ★★★☆☆ (3/5)**
Good range, but the brewing card says "steep 2–5 minutes at 80–100°C" for almost everything. That's not instructions, that's a shrug.

**5. Mei W. — ★★★★☆ (4/5)**
The Silver Needle is delicate and sweet, really nice. Smoky Lapsang was not for me, it tasted like a bonfire. Still glad I tried it.

**6. Dan O. — ★☆☆☆☆ (1/5)**
Box arrived crushed and two of the tins had popped open. Rooibos Chai everywhere. Customer service did send a replacement eventually but it took three emails.

**7. Hannah P. — ★★★★★ (5/5)**
Perfect for working out what I actually like. Turns out I'm a Sencha Spring person. Ordering a full bag now.

**8. Raj K. — ★★★☆☆ (3/5)**
Tea quality is genuinely good. Packaging is a lot. Plastic window, foam insert, paper wrap, outer box. For a brand that talks about the hedgerow on every page, it's a bit much.

**9. Chloe B. — ★★★★☆ (4/5)**
Hedgerow Mint is the freshest mint tea I've had. Lost a star because there was only one sachet of it and five of the Morning Ember, which I'd have happily swapped.

**10. Femi A. — ★★☆☆☆ (2/5)**
I couldn't tell which sachet was which. The labels are tiny and the Oolong Mist and Sencha Spring look identical once they're out of the tin.

**11. Lucy M. — ★★★★★ (5/5)**
Arrived in two days, beautifully wrapped, smelled amazing when I opened it. Chamomile Honey has become my bedtime ritual.

**12. Owen T. — ★★★☆☆ (3/5)**
Fine. Nothing blew me away. The Ginger Lemon was the best of the bunch, nicely punchy. The Hibiscus Berry was very sour.

**13. Sophie G. — ★★★★☆ (4/5)**
Really lovely selection and I liked the little tasting notes card. Wish there was a bit more explanation of what oolong actually is for beginners like me.

**14. Marcus J. — ★☆☆☆☆ (1/5)**
Ordered on the 2nd for a birthday on the 9th. Arrived on the 16th. Tracking said "in transit" for a week straight. Never again for anything time sensitive.

**15. Ellie C. — ★★★★★ (5/5)**
The Earl Grey Lavender is dangerous, I've gone through all three sachets in two days. Please sell it on its own.

**16. Kwame D. — ★★★★☆ (4/5)**
Great teas, great value for the variety. The tins are cute but a nightmare to reopen, I've bent two lids already.

**17. Rosie F. — ★★★☆☆ (3/5)**
The Smoky Lapsang and the Jasmine Pearl were packed next to each other and the jasmine came out tasting faintly of smoke. Separate them please.

**18. Ben W. — ★★☆☆☆ (2/5)**
The instructions say to rinse the Oolong Mist first. Rinse it how? For how long? With what temperature water? I just ended up making a weak cup and pouring it away.

**19. Nadia H. — ★★★★★ (5/5)**
One of the nicest gift boxes I've received. Every tea was good, even the ones I didn't expect to like. Rooibos Chai with a splash of oat milk is wonderful.

**20. Pete S. — ★★★★☆ (4/5)**
Solid box. Morning Ember holds up to milk and sugar, which is all I ask. Shipping was slow but they did warn me at checkout.
