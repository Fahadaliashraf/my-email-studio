# Kettlebird Tea Sampler Box: customer reviews (6 of 6)

> Made-up practice data. Kettlebird and every reviewer below are fictional.

**101. Alice B. — ★★★★★ (5/5)**
Delightful. Jasmine Pearl and Oolong Mist are both top notch. I've already ordered the full-size tins.

**102. Zain H. — ★★★☆☆ (3/5)**
Tea is good, but I agree with others: the brewing instructions are confusing. Different temperatures on the card, the tin and the website.

**103. Matilda O. — ★★★★☆ (4/5)**
Chamomile Honey is lovely before bed. The box itself is sturdy and pretty. I knocked a star off for the slow shipping, about eight days.

**104. Seb K. — ★★☆☆☆ (2/5)**
Only got one sachet of Silver Needle, which is the most expensive tea in the box and the one I was most excited about. Felt like a tease.

**105. Clara J. — ★★★★★ (5/5)**
Such a joy to open. Every tea had its own little story card. Earl Grey Lavender and Hedgerow Mint are my two new favourites.

**106. Ollie W. — ★★★★☆ (4/5)**
Rooibos Chai is spot on. Spicy but not overpowering. The rest were good too, just wish there'd been more than three of each.

**107. Naomi R. — ★★★☆☆ (3/5)**
Really pretty packaging that I felt guilty throwing away. Can it be recycled? The box doesn't say, so it's sat in my hallway.

**108. Hugo E. — ★☆☆☆☆ (1/5)**
Arrived with the seal broken on three tins. No idea if it was tampered with or just badly packed. Didn't feel safe drinking it.

**109. Iris T. — ★★★★★ (5/5)**
I'm a picky tea drinker and I liked nearly everything. The Sencha Spring is sweet and fresh, the Morning Ember is a proper brew. Fast shipping too.

**110. Elliot M. — ★★★★☆ (4/5)**
Loved the Ginger Lemon Rescue. The box was a bit squashed on arrival but the tins protected the tea. Would order again.

**111. Mariam A. — ★★☆☆☆ (2/5)**
The instructions told me to steep the Silver Needle for "up to 7 minutes". I did, and it was bitter and stewed. Found out later 3 minutes is plenty.

**112. Jude C. — ★★★★☆ (4/5)**
Lovely tea, lovely box. I'd prefer the option to swap out the Lapsang for another sachet of Jasmine Pearl. Otherwise great.

**113. Bella N. — ★★★★★ (5/5)**
I bought this for a slow Sunday and made a whole afternoon of it. Each tea was a little treat. Arrived quickly, beautifully packed.

**114. Ravi S. — ★★★☆☆ (3/5)**
Okay box. The black teas were very good, the herbal ones were weak. Hibiscus Berry tasted mostly like sweetened water.

**115. Willow F. — ★★★★☆ (4/5)**
Hedgerow Mint was the best surprise. Gentle and fresh. One star off because the courier took over a week and didn't give a delivery window.

**116. Aaron L. — ★★☆☆☆ (2/5)**
Didn't realise until I opened it that most of the teas are in sachets, not loose leaf. The website photos show loose leaf. Bit misleading.

**117. Jessica P. — ★★★★★ (5/5)**
I've given this box to four friends now. Everyone's had a different favourite, which I think is the sign of a good sampler. Mine is Oolong Mist.

**118. Noah G. — ★★★★☆ (4/5)**
Very good teas. The little kettle icons on the card took me a minute to figure out but once I did it was quite clever. A key would help.

**119. Tilly H. — ★★★☆☆ (3/5)**
Earl Grey Lavender is beautiful. Smoky Lapsang is a crime. Everything else was perfectly nice. Packaging was over the top.

**120. Felix Y. — ★☆☆☆☆ (1/5)**
Took 16 days to arrive, the box was soaked through, and customer service took a week to reply with a copy-paste apology. The tea might be great but I'll never know.
