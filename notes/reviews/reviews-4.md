# Kettlebird Tea Sampler Box: customer reviews (4 of 6)

> Made-up practice data. Kettlebird and every reviewer below are fictional.

**61. Charlotte E. — ★★★★★ (5/5)**
The best part was finding a tea I'd never have picked off a shelf. Oolong Mist, who knew. Lovely box, quick delivery.

**62. Jamal W. — ★★★☆☆ (3/5)**
Rooibos Chai is fantastic. I'd give that five stars. The box as a whole is a three, because there's too much black tea and not enough of the interesting stuff.

**63. Freya H. — ★★★★☆ (4/5)**
Looks gorgeous on the shelf. Teas are good. I'd love the brewing instructions printed on the sachets themselves, I never have the card to hand.

**64. Dominic B. — ★★☆☆☆ (2/5)**
Paid for express shipping, got it in six days. Asked for the express fee back and was told it "isn't guaranteed". Then why charge for it?

**65. Sienna P. — ★★★★★ (5/5)**
I've bought this box three times now as gifts and everyone loves it. Chamomile Honey and Earl Grey Lavender are always the favourites.

**66. Ahmed Y. — ★★★★☆ (4/5)**
Hibiscus Berry is tart and bright, great with a spoon of honey. The Lapsang was too smoky for me. Overall a fun box.

**67. Abigail S. — ★★★☆☆ (3/5)**
I really wanted to love it. The teas were nice but nothing felt special, and three sachets each doesn't give you much chance to get the brew right.

**68. Ethan V. — ★☆☆☆☆ (1/5)**

**69. Molly G. — ★★★★★ (5/5)**
Absolutely lovely. Smelled amazing the moment I opened it. Sencha Spring and Silver Needle are both delicious and very fresh.

**70. Theo K. — ★★★★☆ (4/5)**
Great teas. Instructions say "use slightly cooler water for green teas". How much cooler? I'm not a mind reader. Found a good temperature eventually.

**71. Lily A. — ★★☆☆☆ (2/5)**
I'm sad to give this two stars because the tea itself was good. But it took 12 days and arrived after my friend's birthday, so I gave her an IOU.

**72. Reuben T. — ★★★★☆ (4/5)**

**73. Maya C. — ★★★★★ (5/5)**
Hedgerow Mint is so fresh it's almost sweet. The whole box felt thoughtful, right down to the handwritten thank-you note.

**74. Oscar L. — ★★★☆☆ (3/5)**

**75. Hana M. — ★★★★☆ (4/5)**

**77. Scarlett R. — ★★★★★ (5/5)**

**78. Isaac F. — ★★★★☆ (4/5)**
