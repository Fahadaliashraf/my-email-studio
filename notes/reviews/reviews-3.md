# Kettlebird Tea Sampler Box: customer reviews (3 of 6)

> Made-up practice data. Kettlebird and every reviewer below are fictional.

**41. Olivia D. — ★★★★☆ (4/5)**
Earl Grey Lavender is gorgeous, really floral without tasting like soap. The Morning Ember was a bit forgettable but that's fine for a sampler.

**42. Kieran M. — ★★☆☆☆ (2/5)**
I'm sure the tea is lovely but the instructions were so vague I think I ruined half of it. "Brew to taste" is not helpful when you've never had white tea before.

**43. Jasmine T. — ★★★★★ (5/5)**
Yes, I bought it for the Jasmine Pearl because of my name. No regrets. Every tea was well chosen and the box is nice enough to keep.

**44. Alex G. — ★★★☆☆ (3/5)**
Middle of the road. Packaging was gorgeous but excessive. Delivery was on time. Teas ranged from great (Sencha Spring) to meh (Hibiscus Berry).

**45. Holly W. — ★★★★☆ (4/5)**
Hedgerow Mint after dinner is my new favourite thing. I've already run out. Two sachets was not enough, please put more in.

**46. Vikram P. — ★☆☆☆☆ (1/5)**
Ordered the sampler, received a single tin of Rooibos Chai and a note saying the rest was "on its way". It never came. Refunded after two weeks of chasing.

**47. Lauren H. — ★★★★★ (5/5)**
Lovely from start to finish. The tasting card has a little space to write your own notes on each tea, which I really enjoyed doing with my partner.

**48. Ibrahim N. — ★★★☆☆ (3/5)**
The Smoky Lapsang is genuinely excellent if you like that sort of thing, which I do. Shame there was only one sachet when there were four of the Chamomile.

**49. Megan R. — ★★★★☆ (4/5)**
Took a week to ship which was annoying, but it was worth the wait. Silver Needle is so delicate, I didn't know tea could be this subtle.

**50. Tyler J. — ★★☆☆☆ (2/5)**
The tins are lovely but they arrived scratched and one was dented badly enough that the lid won't close. Not great for a gift.

**51. Esther K. — ★★★★★ (5/5)**
I'm a long-time tea drinker and I was impressed. The Oolong Mist in particular is very good quality. Shipping was fast too.

**52. Will A. — ★★★★☆ (4/5)**
Good box. My only complaint is the brewing guide uses grams and I have no idea how many grams are in a sachet. Just tell me one sachet per cup.

**53. Niamh O. — ★★★☆☆ (3/5)**
Ginger Lemon was great. Rooibos Chai was great. The rest I could take or leave. A decent gift but I wouldn't buy it for myself again.

**54. Leo S. — ★★★★★ (5/5)**
Wonderful. Arrived beautifully packed, every tin full, every tea fresh. My partner and I did a little tasting evening with it.

**55. Ayesha F. — ★★☆☆☆ (2/5)**
The jasmine tasted of smoke. The mint tasted of chai. Everything is packed so close together that the smells mix. Really took away from it.

**56. Matt C. — ★★★★☆ (4/5)**
Morning Ember is now my daily tea. Box arrived later than promised but in good shape. Would like a bigger version of the sampler.

**57. Eva L. — ★★★☆☆ (3/5)**
Lots of plastic for an "eco" brand. Each sachet individually wrapped, inside a tin, inside a sleeve, inside a box. The tea was good though.

**58. Samuel I. — ★★★★★ (5/5)**
Got this for Christmas last year and finally wrote a review. Still thinking about the Sencha Spring. Already bought two full bags since.

**59. Phoebe N. — ★★★★☆ (4/5)**
So many lovely teas. The brewing card is in very small grey print on cream paper, which I really struggled to read. Larger text please.

**60. Arjun D. — ★☆☆☆☆ (1/5)**
Two weeks to arrive. No tracking updates. When it came the box was crushed and the Earl Grey had spilled loose inside. Not worth the hassle.
