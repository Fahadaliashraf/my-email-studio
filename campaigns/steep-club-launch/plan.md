# Steep Club launch: campaign plan

**Status:** Draft plan. No emails have been sent or scheduled.
**Written:** 8 November 2026
**Runs:** Monday 7 December 2026 to Sunday 3 January 2027 (4 weeks)

> **Before you use this plan:** anything in [square brackets] is not confirmed yet. The voice guide says we must not promise prices, delivery dates, discounts or box contents until the team confirms them. Fill these in first. See "Facts we need" at the end.

## 1. Campaign overview

- **Name:** Your Monthly Pause
- **In one sentence:** Invite our waitlist to join the Steep Club, a monthly box of loose-leaf tea, with calm, friendly emails written for each type of reader.
- **Main goal:** 300 waitlist members start a Steep Club subscription between 7 December 2026 and 3 January 2027.
- **Other goals:**
  - Learn which of the three groups joins most, so we know who to write for next.
  - Keep unsubscribes low (under 0.5% per email), so the list stays healthy for later campaigns.
  - Get the first members talking about their box, to help with the next campaign.

**A note on the goal:** 300 out of 2,000 is 15% of the waitlist. That is a big ask for one month, even from a warm list. It's possible because these people asked to hear from us, but we should treat it as a stretch target and check progress every week (see section 7).

**A note on timing:** the campaign runs over Christmas and New Year. That helps with gift buyers and with "fresh start" messages in the new year. But inboxes are busy, and Christmas delivery is a risk. We must not promise a box arrives before Christmas unless the team confirms it.

## 2. Who we are writing to

About 2,000 people who joined the waitlist. They are busy, aged 25 to 45, and want a calm moment in their day. They already know who we are, so they are close to deciding. The job of these emails is to help them say yes, not to introduce us.

| Group | Who they are | What they want | What might stop them | What they need to hear |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Tea-curious beginners** | New to loose-leaf tea. Like the idea but feel unsure. | An easy, low-effort way to start. | "I don't know how to brew loose-leaf." "What if I don't like it?" | It's simple. We show you how. You can [pause or cancel anytime]. |
| **Gift buyers** | Want to give the box to someone else. | A thoughtful gift that's easy to send. | "Will it arrive in time?" "Can I choose how many months?" | How gifting works, [gift lengths], and [delivery timing]. |
| **Daily tea drinkers** | Already drink tea every day. Know what they like. | Something new to discover in their cup. | "Will it be better than what I already buy?" | What's in the box, where it comes from, and the variety each month. |

**Where they spend time:** their inbox. This plan is email only, as agreed.

**Do we know who is in which group?** This is the first thing to check (see section 9). If the waitlist isn't tagged by group, the launch email asks readers to pick the one that sounds like them, and later emails follow their answer.

## 3. Key messages

**Core message:**
> The Steep Club brings a new loose-leaf tea to your door each month, so you always have a reason to pause.

**Supporting messages:**

1. **It's easy to start.** Each box comes with simple brewing notes. You don't need to know anything about tea. *(For beginners.)*
2. **It's a gift that keeps arriving.** One gift, a new box each month. *(For gift buyers.)*
3. **There's always something new to discover.** A different tea each month to try in your cup. *(For daily drinkers.)*
4. **You're in control.** [Pause, skip or cancel anytime.] *(For everyone. Only use this once confirmed.)*

**Proof we can use:**

| Message | Proof | Confirmed? |
|---|---|---|
| Easy to start | A short brewing guide in every box | [Check with team] |
| Gift that keeps arriving | Gift options of [3 / 6 / 12 months] | [Check with team] |
| Something new to discover | The tea list for the first box | [Check with team] |
| You're in control | Pause / cancel rules | [Check with team] |

**How the message is ordered in each email:**
1. Why care? A calm moment in a busy day.
2. What is it? The Steep Club, a monthly box of loose-leaf tea.
3. Why us? We know tea well, and we keep it simple and friendly.
4. What to do? One clear, gentle link: "Join the Steep Club" or "Start your box".

**Voice reminders for every email** (from `brand/voice.md`):
- Calm, warm, curious. Short, friendly sentences.
- No pushy words: no "hurry", "last chance", "limited time", "don't miss out" or "exclusive". The last email is a gentle reminder, not a countdown.
- No health claims. Say "a pause in your day", never that the tea reduces stress or detoxes.
- No "hand-picked" or "small-batch" until a supplier confirms it.
- At most one exclamation mark per email. No ALL CAPS.
- Sign off with exactly: "Happy steeping, / The Kettlebird team". No P.S.

## 4. Channel plan

**Email only.** The waitlist is our warmest audience and email reaches them directly. It costs almost nothing per send.

| Channel | Why it fits | What we send | Effort |
|---|---|---|---|
| Email to the waitlist | They asked to hear from us. Close to deciding. | 6 emails over 4 weeks: 3 to everyone, 3 written for each group | Medium (writing 12 versions in total) |
| Sign-up page (website) | Every email links here. It must be live first. | One page explaining the box, price and how to join | Medium (one-off) |

**How often:** no more than 2 emails a week to any one person.

**Who stops getting emails:** anyone who joins the Steep Club stops getting these launch emails straight away. They move to a welcome email instead. Anyone who unsubscribes is removed.

**Not in this plan:** paid ads, social media, PR. We can add them in a later campaign if we want to reach people beyond the waitlist.

## 7. How we measure success

**Main measure:** number of waitlist members who start a subscription. **Target: 300 by Sun 3 January 2027.**

**Weekly check points** (most people join in the first week of a launch, so the targets are front-loaded):

| By end of | Total subscriptions | If we're behind |
|---|---|---|
| Week 1 (Sun 13 Dec) | 120 | Check which group is slow and rewrite its email 4 |
| Week 2 (Sun 20 Dec) | 200 | Test a new subject line for email 5 |
| Week 3 (Sun 27 Dec) | 250 | Make email 6 clearer about how to start |
| Week 4 (Sun 3 Jan) | 300 | Write up what we learned |

**Other measures:**

| Measure | Target | How we track it |
|---|---|---|
| Open rate | 40% or more | Email tool reports |
| Click rate | 5% or more | Email tool reports |
| Subscriptions by group | Know which group joins most | Group tag, or tracking links per group version |
| Gift subscriptions | Count them separately | Shop order data |
| Unsubscribe rate | Under 0.5% per email | Email tool reports |

**Tracking:** each link in each email gets its own tracking tag (for example `?utm_campaign=steep-club-launch&utm_content=email2-beginners`), so we can see which email and which version led to each sign-up.

**Reporting:** a quick check every Monday morning before that week's email, and a full report after the campaign ends.

## 9. Risks and how we handle them

| Risk | What could happen | What we do |
|---|---|---|
| **Facts aren't confirmed in time** | We can't finish drafts without price, contents and delivery dates. | Ask the team for these by Fri 20 Nov. Write drafts with [placeholders] until then. |
| **The waitlist isn't sorted into groups** | We can't send the right version to the right person. | Add a "Which sounds like you?" question to email 1. Anyone who doesn't answer gets the daily drinker version, which works for everyone. |
| **Christmas delivery** | Gift buyers expect a box before Christmas and it doesn't arrive. | Only mention Christmas delivery if the team confirms a cut-off date. Otherwise offer a gift message or card they can give on the day. |
| **Busy holiday inboxes** | Emails get lost and fewer people open them. | Send on Mondays and Thursdays, skip Christmas week, keep subject lines short and calm. |
| **The goal is too high** | We fall short of 300. | Weekly check points (section 7) show early if we're behind, so we can change emails 4 to 6. |
| **Voice slips under pressure** | A draft says "last chance" or makes a health claim. | Check every draft with `marketing:brand-review` before the team sees it. |
